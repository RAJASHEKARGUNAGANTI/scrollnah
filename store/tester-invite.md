# Tester invite messages

Google needs at least 12 testers who stay in the closed test for 14 days in a row.
Invite 15 to 20 people, so a few dropping out doesn't reset the count.
Replace `[OPT-IN LINK]` with the link from Play Console (Closed testing > Testers > "Join on the web"),
which appears after the first release is published to the closed testing track.

## WhatsApp / short message

Hi! I made an Android app called ScrollNah that helps you scroll less, with a cute mascot that nudges you when you hit your limit. Before it goes live on the Play Store, Google needs 12 people to test it for 2 weeks. Could you help? 🙏

1. Send me the Gmail address you use on your Android phone, and I'll add you
2. Open this link on your phone and tap "Become a tester": [OPT-IN LINK]
3. Install ScrollNah from the Play Store page that opens
4. Please keep it installed for 14 days (you can just set one limit and forget about it)

It's free, has no ads, and everything stays on your phone. Thank you!

## Email

Subject: Can you help me test my app for 2 weeks?

Hi,

I built an Android app called ScrollNah. It helps you cut down on endless scrolling: you set a daily limit for apps like Instagram or YouTube, and a friendly mascot (Blobby or Bloomy) nudges you when you reach it.

Before I can publish it on the Google Play Store, Google asks for at least 12 people to test it for 14 days in a row. It would really help if you could be one of them.

What to do:
1. Reply with the Gmail address you use on your Android phone, so I can add you to the testers.
2. When I send the link, open it on your phone and tap "Become a tester".
3. Install ScrollNah from the Play Store page that opens, with the same Google account.
4. Keep it installed for 14 days. Using it a little is great, but even just setting one limit helps.

Good to know:
- It's free, with no ads and no account. Everything stays on your phone.
- It asks for Usage access (to count your minutes) and permission to show its Pause screen over other apps.
- If anything looks odd, tell me, or use Settings > Send feedback in the app.

Thank you so much!

## After someone joins

- Check in Play Console that they appear as opted in (Closed testing > Testers).
- A day or two before the 14 days end, remind everyone not to uninstall or leave the test yet.
