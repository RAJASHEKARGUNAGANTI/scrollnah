## Full description (3257/4000)
```
Spending more time scrolling than you meant to? ScrollNah helps you cut back, without guilt trips. Pick the apps that eat your day, give each one a daily budget, and let a friendly mascot keep you company.

MEET YOUR MASCOT
Choose Blobby, an orange blob, or Bloomy, a little plant in a pot. Both react to your day: thriving when you're doing great, a bit worried as you get close to your limit, and melting (or wilting) when you go over.

DAILY LIMITS THAT ACTUALLY STOP YOU
Set a budget for any app, from 5 minutes to 3 hours, with a different one for weekends if you like. Pick how strict ScrollNah should be:
• Chill: a gentle nudge when you hit your limit
• Firm: a Pause screen with one slow breath, then 5 more minutes if you really need them
• Beast: the app locks until midnight. No extra minutes.
If a limited app keeps playing a video in a small floating window, ScrollNah pauses that too.

A PAUSE THAT HELPS
When you open a limited app over its budget, your mascot asks "Woah. Instagram again?" and gives you a 3-second breath to decide. Tap why you're opening it (bored, replying to someone, pure habit, work) and see your patterns later.

BREAK REMINDERS
Long stretch on one app? Get a nudge after 20 minutes (or your own time), as a notification, a mascot bubble, or both. "Take a break" or "5 more min", it's up to you.

GROW A GARDEN
Every day under your limits, a bud on Bloomy grows a little. Seven days in a row and it opens into a new flower. Four flowers fill a pot, and finished pots stay in your garden forever.

YOUR WEEK, IN DETAIL
• Where the time went: minutes and visits for each app, compared with last week
• Tap any day in the chart to see that day's apps
• Your "scroll o'clock": when you scroll most, and your first scroll of the day
• Did the nudges work? How often you said "Nah, I'm good"
• A month calendar and a 4-week trend
• Your mascot's verdict on the week
• Week in review: swipeable cards you can share as a picture

REELS, SHORTS AND WEBSITES (OPTIONAL)
Limit just the endless-video part of Instagram and YouTube, or a daily number of Reels and Shorts, and see how many you scrolled each day and week. This optional tracker uses Android's Accessibility service, is off until you turn it on, and ScrollNah explains exactly what it reads first. It only saves minutes and a count, on your phone.

MORE
• Focus mode: lock your apps for 30 minutes to 2 hours, also from a Quick Settings tile
• Bedtime: pause your time-sinks at night
• Home-screen widgets, including an animated mascot that lives on your wallpaper
• Everyday comparisons ("That's dinner cooked from scratch")
• Light and dark themes, and works with TalkBack
• Small: about 1.5 MB

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No analytics. Everything stays on your phone, and ScrollNah has no internet access at all.

PERMISSIONS, IN PLAIN WORDS
• Usage access: to count your minutes on the apps you choose
• Notifications: for reminders
• Display over other apps: to show the Pause screen and the mascot bubble
• Run in the background: so reminders show up on time
• Accessibility (optional, off by default): only for the Reels, Shorts and website tracker

ScrollNah is not affiliated with Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat or any other app it helps you limit.
```
